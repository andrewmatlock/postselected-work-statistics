Full review of every script and the statistical approach, prompted by the model
handoff (prior runs coded by Claude Opus; audited by Claude Fable 5). Verdict up
front: **the physics and the index conventions are sound everywhere it matters;
the statistics had four real defects, all now fixed in `analyze_v2.py` and
locked into `PREREGISTRATION_v2.json`; one experiment script was partially
circular and one was missing, both now repaired; one new methodological caveat
was discovered in the process.**

## Verified correct (checked independently, by hand and numerically)

  across exp4, poke12, ctc_experiment, run_highstat.
- Qiskit bit ordering through the whole chain (ancilla = leftmost bit; `int(bits[1:],2)`
  = 2·q1+q0 matches the state-prep convention `i&2→x(1), i&1→x(0)`).
- Halmos dilation block structure and unitarity; heralded sub-block proportionality.

  (PR[a,b] = |M[a,b]|²/e^{u_a} — correct).
- The efficacy identity ⟨e^{−βW}⟩ = N_R/N_F (rederived: Σ_i |C_ji|² = ‖C†|j⟩‖²).
- Per-cell error bars S² = (1−p)/n_cell for ln p (correct binomial delta method).
- The 2026-08-01 job archive: **all 81 jobs recovered with counts** (33 shallow +
  8 deep-20k + 40 deep-100k), reconstructed into `data/` with shot totals verified.

## Defects found and fixed
1. **Biased-past z-statistic mis-modeled** (ctc_experiment, ctc_shallow,
   analyze_counts/weighted/shallow): treats the reweighted marginal as a multinomial
   draw of N_accepted samples. The measured quantities are four binomial acceptances;
   proper delta-method propagation gives different per-component significances.
   Consequences, rerun on raw data: shallow runs 6.2σ→11.4σ and 13.3σ→24.9σ;
   the deep-100k headline **114.6σ→182σ** (the flawed formula reproduces 114.6
   exactly, so the note's number was faithful to the formula — and understated).
   Every published significance was *conservative in the max*; but the formula also
   mis-ranks components, and v1's criterion gated on the deviation magnitude at
   input 0 — precisely the component with the least statistical power. The
   model-free statistic is acceptance non-uniformity: χ²(3) = 354 / 1668 / ~10⁵
   across the three runs.
2. **The v1 ratio gate is a max-statistic on a systematic floor.** Measured 0.401
   (100k shots) vs 0.354 (20k): more data made it *worse*, because it measures the
   device's stable systematic, not convergence to theory (shot-noise floor at 20k
   is 0.04). Global statistics are stable across runs: VE 0.979/0.971,
   r(D, D_th) 0.987/0.985. v2 gates on VE, r, and fitted-vs-predicted potentials;
   ratio is reported, non-gating.
3. **exp4 was partially circular**: it constructed D as u_j − v_i directly, so its
   1e-16 separability was tautological. Rewritten: the effective operator is now
   built by explicit postselected-teleportation amplitudes (independent of the
   partial-trace formula, asserted equal), and D is computed from forward/reverse
   conditional probabilities before testing the identity. (The identity itself is
   one-line algebra; the meaningful checks were always poke12's joint construction
   and the sampled runs — now exp4 is a real check too.)
4. **exp5 was missing** (predates the repo; README referenced it). Regenerated;
   reproduces the logged claims under the correct convention (min residual 0.397
   vs logged 0.41; medians 0.94–1.30 vs logged 0.96–1.28).

## New finding: the adjoint-renormalization artifact (doctrine-level)
Regenerating exp5 with the *wrong* reverse convention exposed an identity: for any
channel {K_a}, Σ_a|K_a[j,i]|² read column-normalized is the forward accepted-run
conditional and read row-normalized is the adjoint-reverse accepted-run conditional
— so renormalizing both directions per input **factorizes every channel exactly**,
CTC or not (verified: max residual 7e-16 over 50 random channels). Consequences:
- The detection doctrine must specify the **physical-inverse reverse protocol**
  (run the inverse dynamics with fresh environment — what the hardware does).
- Factorization *per se* is even weaker evidence than §4 of the note stated: the
  decisive statistic is factorization **into the predicted, dynamics-dependent
  potentials** (u_j = ln‖C†|j⟩‖²), which no convention artifact and no reference
  equilibrium reproduces. v2's criteria gate on exactly this.
- Note §8(2) ("reverse-process conventions not exhausted") is hereby partially
  discharged: the adjoint convention is now mapped — it is degenerate.

## Design lesson from the two circuits
The deep circuit has enormous bias contrast (acceptances 0.15–0.84; the 182σ
exhibit) but noise-destroyed factorization; the shallow circuit has clean
factorization but mild bias contrast (by deliberate ansatz choice). v1's criteria
demanded both exhibits from the shallow circuit. v2 tests the biased past on the
shallow circuit via the statistics that are actually powered there (uniformity χ²,
attenuation coefficient), and the deep-circuit 182σ result stands as the magnitude
exhibit, now reproducible from `data/ctc_highstat_counts.json`.

## QPU economics (why v2 costs ~90 s, not 600)
One 8-pub job at 20k shots/input measured 45 s on ibm_marrakesh. The v1-era
per-batch submission pattern (73 jobs on 2026-08-01) burned the window on per-job
overhead. More shots buy nothing for the factorization gates (systematic-dominated);
20k/input is the calibrated operating point. v2: one job per arm, two arms
(marrakesh primary, fez replication), ≤ 120 s total, leaving ≥ 400 s of the window.
